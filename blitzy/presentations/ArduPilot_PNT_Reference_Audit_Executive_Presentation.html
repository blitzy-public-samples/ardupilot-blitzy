Risks and Mitigations

What could go wrong with the pack, and what prevents it

Risks and controls

Fidelity is enforced, not trusted

Each content-fidelity risk and the control that closes it
Risk to the pack Control in place
A figure gets recomputed and differs Quote, never recompute — audit figures print exactly as stated, and figures about the pack are labelled as such
A copied reference gets mangled Identifiers taken from the catalogued data, not from extracted text
A finding gets dropped A completeness check gates the exhaustive deck and prints its own reconciliation
The brief outgrows its cap A slide-count check refuses to save the brief above ten slides
The source gets altered The source document is fingerprinted before and after, and left unchanged
Visuals fail without a network Guarded start-up and system font fallbacks keep this deck readable offline
